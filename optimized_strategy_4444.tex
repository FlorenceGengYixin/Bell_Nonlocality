\section{Conventions and inequality}
\label{app:4444_strategy}
The local Hilbert space is $\mathbb C^2\otimes\mathbb C^4\otimes\mathbb C^2$,
with tensor order $A,B,C$. Bob has four settings with 4 outcomes each.
Alice and Charlie each have two binary settings. All setting and outcome
indices start at zero. The state-vector basis is
\[
\mathcal E_{8a+2b+c}=|abc\rangle,\qquad a,c\in\{0,1\},\quad b\in\{0,\ldots,3\}.
\]
Probabilities follow the Born rule,
\[
P(abc|xyz)=\operatorname{Tr}\!\left[\rho
 (A_{a|x}\otimes B_{b|y}\otimes C_{c|z})\right].
\]
The marginal notation is $P_{AB}(ab|xy)$ and $P_{BC}(bc|yz)$.
The integer-scale inequality is
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
S_{4444}={}&2P_{A}(0|0)-P_{B}(0|0)\\
&+P_{B}(1|0)+P_{B}(2|1)\\
&-P_{AB}(02|00)-2P_{AB}(02|01)\\
&+P_{AB}(00|10)-P_{AB}(01|10)\\
&+P_{AB}(00|12)-P_{AB}(02|12)\\
&+P_{BC}(00|00)-P_{BC}(10|01)\\
&+P_{BC}(20|10)-P_{BC}(20|11)\\
&-P_{BC}(00|20)-P_{BC}(10|20)\\
&+P_{BC}(10|21)+P_{BC}(20|21)\geq -1.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
The integer coefficient vector has $\ell_1$ norm $20$. We use
$S_{\rm norm}=S_{4444}/20=c^Tp$, with $\|c\|_1=1$ and
$\beta_{\rm HI}=-1/20$, independently checked by the HI support LP.
For the full-precision strategy below,
\[
\begin{aligned}
S_{\rm norm}&\simeq -0.050021280577071,\\
S_{4444}&\simeq -1.000425611541411,\\
\Delta_{L_1}=\beta_{\rm HI}-S_{\rm norm}&\simeq 2.128057707057\times10^{-5}.
\end{aligned}
\]
This is the best explicit realization found in the four-start fixed-witness
search from the lifted qutrit strategy. Subsequent outer-loop searches did
not find a substantive improvement.
The inequality has no $y=3$ coefficient. The fourth POVM is a valid
completion, but does not affect the reported violation. This strategy does
not establish an advantage from adding a fourth setting. No global
quantum-optimality claim is made.

All displayed entries are rounded to ten decimal places. Full-precision
arrays are supplied separately for numerical calculations.

\section{Numerical checks}
The full-precision maximum POVM residual is $1.332267629550\times10^{-15}$,
and the minimum effect eigenvalue is $-9.223457963897\times10^{-17}$
(zero within floating-point accuracy). Full Born probabilities were checked
for non-negativity and normalization. The rounded state, renormalized, and
the displayed effects with final outcomes reconstructed give
\[
\Delta_{L_1}^{\rm displayed}\simeq 2.128056942936\times10^{-5}.
\]
Rounding can create tiny negative effect eigenvalues; full-precision
arrays are the reference for positivity checks.
